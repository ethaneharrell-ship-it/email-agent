personal-email-agent

A private, single-user email assistant operated by Implement4.

personal-email-agent is a private tool used by one person to organize their own mailboxes. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign up.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's own mailboxes so they can see all their mail in one place, search it, and get short summaries at set times of day.
  • With Google, it requests read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change, or delete mail through that access.
  • It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a hosted service.

Read the privacy policy for exactly what data is used and how.
