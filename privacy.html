Privacy policy: personal-email-agent

Last updated: 18 November 2026

personal-email-agent ("the app") is a private, single-user tool operated by Implement4. It is used only by its owner, to organize the owner's own mailboxes. It is not available to anyone else.

What data the app accesses

  • Google user data: with the owner's consent, the app uses the Gmail API with the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly to read the owner's own Gmail messages, labels, and mailbox counts.
  • The app also connects to the owner's other mailboxes (for example, Zoho Mail and iCloud Mail) with read-only access.
  • The app does not access contacts, calendars, Drive files, or any other Google data.

How the data is used

  • To show the owner their own mail in one place, to let them search it, and to prepare short summaries and sorting suggestions for the owner.
  • The app cannot send, modify, or delete Gmail messages through the read-only access.
  • The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles of anyone.

Where the data is stored

  • Mail is stored only on the owner's own computer, in an encrypted storage area. It is not stored on Implement4 servers or any other hosted service.
  • Sign-in tokens and passwords are encrypted with the computer's hardware security chip (TPM).
  • Encrypted backups are kept on the owner's own network storage device.

Sharing

  • Data is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone for their own use.
  • If the owner turns on AI summaries, the text of selected messages may be sent to an AI service provider (Anthropic's Claude API) only to produce summaries and suggestions for the owner. Under the provider's commercial terms, this data is not used to train AI models.
  • No human other than the owner reads the data, except if required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Mail is kept while the owner uses the app. A message deleted at the provider is removed from the app's storage after 12 months.
  • The owner can disconnect at any time by removing the app's access at myaccount.google.com/connections. The stored sign-in token is then deleted from the owner's computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Implement4 through implement4.com.
